- Refined Deep-SAR Oil Spill (SOS), Zenodo record 15298010, is published under
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Attribution to Denis Zuenko and Ilvira
  Khaidarova is required: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15298010>. This record
  is a corrected release of the Deep-SAR Oil Spill dataset of Q. Zhu, Y. Zhang,
  Z. Li, X. Yan, Q. Guan, Y. Zhong, L. Zhang, and D. Li, "Oil spill contextual

  <https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2021.3115492>, which should be cited alongside

  <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8253899>, and

The released model weights were trained from these datasets. Their inclusion does
not remove the attribution obligations associated with the source data.

## Packaged imagery and coastline

- Demo GeoTIFFs contain modified Copernicus Sentinel-1 data exported through
  Google Earth Engine. Retain the statement "Contains modified Copernicus
  Sentinel data" and follow the applicable Copernicus data terms when
  redistributing them.
- Natural Earth 1:10m coastline data is public domain. Citation is optional but
  appreciated: <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>.
